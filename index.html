❓ How to play

  1. 🖱️ Tap a country in your current continent to challenge it.
  2. 🎚️ Pick a difficulty — harder questions earn more points!
  3. ✅ Answer correctly to conquer the country. Get it wrong and Baron von Blunder steals it! 😈
  4. 🌍 Conquer every country in a continent to unlock the next one.
  5. 🏆 Continent Quest: conquer one continent to win.
    🌐 World Conquest: conquer all six!
  6. 💾 Your progress saves automatically — come back anytime!
