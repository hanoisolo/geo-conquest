          ❓ How to play

          1. 🖱️ Tap a country in your current continent to challenge it.
          2. 🎚️ Pick a difficulty — harder questions earn more points! (😊 Easy can't storm the Baron's fortresses — his countries need 🤔 Medium or 🧠 Hard.)
          3. 🚩 Questions come as text, flags to recognize, or find-it-on-the-map challenges!
          4. ✅ Answer correctly to conquer the country. Get it wrong and Baron von Blunder steals it! 😈
          5. 🌍 Conquer every country in a continent to unlock the next one.
          6. 🏆 Continent Quest: conquer one continent to win.
            🌐 World Conquest: conquer all six!
          7. 💾 Your progress saves automatically — come back anytime!
